# COMPREHENSIVE HEALTH INSURANCE POLICY

## POLICY NUMBER: POL-2024-001
## EFFECTIVE DATE: January 1, 2024
## POLICYHOLDER: Sample Healthcare Corp

---

## SECTION 1: COVERAGE OVERVIEW

This health insurance policy provides comprehensive medical coverage for eligible members and their dependents. Coverage includes medical, surgical, hospitalization, preventive care, and prescription drug benefits subject to the terms, conditions, and limitations outlined in this document.

## SECTION 2: MEDICAL COVERAGE

### 2.1 Surgical Procedures
This policy covers medically necessary surgical procedures performed by licensed healthcare providers. Coverage includes but is not limited to:

- Orthopedic surgeries including knee surgery, hip replacement, and spine procedures
- Cardiovascular surgeries including bypass surgery and valve repairs
- General surgeries including appendectomy, gallbladder removal, and hernia repair
- Neurological surgeries including brain tumor removal and spinal cord procedures
- Reconstructive surgeries following accident or illness

**Conditions for Surgical Coverage:**
- Prior authorization is required for all non-emergency surgical procedures
- Surgery must be medically necessary as determined by a licensed physician
- Procedure must be performed by a network provider for maximum benefits
- Second opinion may be required for certain major procedures

### 2.2 Hospitalization
Coverage for inpatient hospital services includes:
- Room and board (semi-private room)
- Nursing care
- Operating room and recovery room charges
- Intensive care unit services
- Emergency room services
- Laboratory and diagnostic tests

### 2.3 Preventive Care
This policy covers 100% of preventive care services including:
- Annual physical examinations
- Immunizations and vaccinations
- Cancer screenings (mammograms, colonoscopies, Pap smears)
- Blood pressure and cholesterol screenings
- Diabetes screenings

## SECTION 3: PRESCRIPTION DRUG COVERAGE

### 3.1 Formulary Coverage
Prescription drugs are covered according to our formulary list:
- Generic drugs: $10 copay
- Brand name drugs (formulary): $30 copay
- Brand name drugs (non-formulary): $50 copay
- Specialty drugs: 20% coinsurance

### 3.2 Prior Authorization Requirements
Certain medications require prior authorization including:
- Specialty medications costing over $1,000 per month
- Non-formulary brand name drugs when generic alternatives exist
- Experimental or investigational drugs

## SECTION 4: DENTAL AND VISION COVERAGE

### 4.1 Dental Coverage
Basic dental coverage includes:
- Preventive services: 100% coverage (cleanings, exams, X-rays)
- Basic restorative: 80% coverage (fillings, extractions)
- Major restorative: 50% coverage (crowns, bridges, root canals)
- Orthodontics: 50% coverage up to $2,000 lifetime maximum

### 4.2 Vision Coverage
Vision benefits include:
- Annual eye exams: 100% coverage
- Frames: $150 allowance every 2 years
- Lenses: $100 allowance annually
- Contact lenses: $150 allowance annually (in lieu of glasses)

## SECTION 5: EXCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS

### 5.1 General Exclusions
This policy does not cover:
- Cosmetic surgery or procedures for aesthetic purposes
- Experimental or investigational treatments not approved by FDA
- Treatment for injuries resulting from illegal activities
- Services not medically necessary
- Treatment for conditions resulting from war or acts of terrorism

### 5.2 Pre-existing Condition Limitations
Pre-existing conditions are covered subject to the following:
- Conditions diagnosed or treated in the 6 months prior to coverage effective date
- Coverage begins after 12-month waiting period
- Waiting period waived if continuous prior coverage for 12 months

## SECTION 6: BENEFIT LIMITS AND DEDUCTIBLES

### 6.1 Annual Deductibles
- Individual deductible: $1,500
- Family deductible: $3,000
- Out-of-pocket maximum (individual): $6,000
- Out-of-pocket maximum (family): $12,000

### 6.2 Coinsurance
After deductible is met:
- In-network services: 80% coverage (member pays 20%)
- Out-of-network services: 60% coverage (member pays 40%)

### 6.3 Annual Benefit Maximums
- Lifetime maximum: Unlimited for essential health benefits
- Dental maximum: $2,000 per year
- Vision maximum: As specified in coverage amounts

## SECTION 7: EMERGENCY SERVICES

### 7.1 Emergency Room Coverage
Emergency room visits are covered at 80% after deductible for:
- Life-threatening conditions
- Severe injury or illness requiring immediate attention
- Conditions where delay in treatment could seriously jeopardize health

### 7.2 Emergency Conditions Include:
- Heart attack or chest pain
- Stroke or severe neurological symptoms
- Severe breathing difficulties
- Major trauma or injuries
- Severe allergic reactions
- Loss of consciousness

## SECTION 8: MENTAL HEALTH AND SUBSTANCE ABUSE

### 8.1 Mental Health Coverage
Mental health services are covered equally to medical services:
- Inpatient psychiatric care: 80% coverage after deductible
- Outpatient therapy: $25 copay per visit
- Psychological testing: 80% coverage after deductible

### 8.2 Substance Abuse Treatment
Coverage for substance abuse treatment includes:
- Detoxification services: 80% coverage
- Inpatient rehabilitation: 80% coverage up to 30 days per year
- Outpatient counseling: $25 copay per visit

## SECTION 9: MATERNITY AND NEWBORN CARE

### 9.1 Pregnancy Coverage
Maternity services are covered including:
- Prenatal care visits: $25 copay per visit
- Delivery and hospital stay: 80% coverage after deductible
- Postpartum care: $25 copay per visit

### 9.2 Newborn Coverage
Newborns are automatically covered for the first 31 days:
- Well-baby visits: 100% coverage
- Immunizations: 100% coverage
- Treatment of illness or injury: Subject to regular benefits

## SECTION 10: CLAIMS AND APPEALS PROCESS

### 10.1 Filing Claims
Claims must be filed within 365 days of service date:
- Use designated claim forms
- Include all required documentation
- Submit to processing address specified

### 10.2 Appeals Process
Members have the right to appeal claim denials:
- Internal appeal must be filed within 180 days
- External review available for medical necessity denials
- Expedited appeals available for urgent situations

## SECTION 11: PROVIDER NETWORK

### 11.1 Network Requirements
Maximum benefits available when using network providers:
- Primary care physicians must be selected from network
- Referrals required for specialist visits
- Emergency care covered at in-network level regardless of provider

### 11.2 Network Provider Responsibilities
Network providers agree to:
- Accept negotiated rates as payment in full
- Obtain required prior authorizations
- Follow established medical management protocols

## SECTION 12: COORDINATION OF BENEFITS

When member has coverage under multiple policies:
- This policy coordinates with other group health plans
- Medicare coordination follows standard guidelines
- No duplicate payments for same services

## SECTION 13: CONTINUATION OF COVERAGE

### 13.1 COBRA Rights
Eligible for continued coverage under COBRA:
- Up to 18 months for termination of employment
- Up to 36 months for other qualifying events
- Premium payment required monthly

### 13.2 Conversion Rights
Individual conversion policy available:
- Must apply within 31 days of group coverage termination
- Coverage may be limited compared to group benefits

## SECTION 14: DEFINITIONS

**Medically Necessary:** Services or supplies that are appropriate and necessary for the symptoms, diagnosis, or treatment of a medical condition and are provided for the diagnosis or direct care of that condition.

**Network Provider:** Healthcare providers who have contracted with our network to provide services at negotiated rates.

**Prior Authorization:** Advance approval required for certain services to ensure they meet medical necessity criteria.

**Emergency Services:** Services provided to evaluate and treat emergency medical conditions in a hospital emergency department.
